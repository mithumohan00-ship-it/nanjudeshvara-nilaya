# Sri Sanjundeshwara Nilaya – Gruhapravesha Invitation 2026
### ಶ್ರೀ ನಂಜುಂಡೇಶ್ವರ ನಿಲಯ – ಗೃಹಪ್ರವೇಶ ಆಹ್ವಾನ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ

A premium, culturally authentic, and elegant digital housewarming invitation website capturing the traditional Kannada Hindu Gruhapravesha ceremony.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Authentic Kannada & South Indian Aesthetics**:
   - Invocations: `॥ ಶ್ರೀ ತೋಟನೂರು ಸಾಂಬು ಕ್ರುಪೆ ॥` and `॥ ಶ್ರೀ ಶ್ರೀ ಹೋಳೆ ಚೌಡೇಶ್ವರಿ ಕ್ರುಪೆ ॥`
   - Detailed vector illustration of **Lord Ganesha**
   - Traditional antique-gold filigree borders, corner ornaments, and sacred motifs
   - Warm ivory, cream, deep maroon (`#6B1724`), and forest green (`#1B4D3E`) traditional color palette

2. **Traditional Digital Invitation Card**:
   - Re-creation of the physical card with ivory paper texture and antique-gold double borders
   - Dual-language view toggle: **ಕನ್ನಡ ಆಹ್ವಾನ (Kannada)** and **English Invitation**
   - Accurate auspicious timings:
     - **Gruhapravesha Ceremony (ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮೀ ಮುಹೂರ್ತ)**: `04:40 AM – 06:40 AM`
     - **Sri Satyanarayana Swamy Puja**: `10:00 AM onwards`
     - **Bhojana – Lunch (ಮಹಾಪ್ರಸಾದ)**: `12:30 PM`
   - One-click **Print / Save Card as PDF** feature formatted for paper printing

3. **Real-time Muhurtha Countdown Timer**:
   - Targets **30 October 2026, 04:40:00 AM IST** (`Asia/Kolkata` timezone)
   - Real-time updates for Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds
   - **Add to Google Calendar** and **Download Apple / iCal (.ics)** integration

4. **Venue & Directions**:
   - Venue: `Nagadevanahalli, Bengaluru – 560 056, Karnataka, India`
   - Interactive embedded Google Map
   - **Get Directions** button linking directly to Google Maps: `https://goo.gl/maps/qCS5dRAVQBboKctw7?g_st=aw`
   - One-click **Copy Address** button with animated toast confirmation

5. **Digital Invitation Sharing**:
   - Direct prefilled **WhatsApp share**
   - **Telegram share**
   - **Copy Link** with instant toast notification
   - **Native Mobile Web Share** (`navigator.share`) for iPhone & Android
   - On-screen **QR Code** for instant phone-to-phone camera scanning
   - Floating quick-share button for effortless sharing

## 🚀 How to Run & View

1. **Directly in Browser**:
   Double click `index.html` or open it with Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

2. **Using any local web server**:
   ```bash
   python3 -m http.server 8080
   ```
   Then open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.
